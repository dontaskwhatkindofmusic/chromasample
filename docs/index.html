Signal settings

Hold a chord button, then play any note as its root. Number keys 1–8 also hold the eight chord buttons. The last modifier held takes priority.

32 kHz mono · compact 4-bit ADPCM
Pitch is detected automatically. Noise and percussion use C4 as their reference. Higher notes play faster, like a classic sampler.

Add to your home screen for a full-screen instrument. Saved sounds stay in this browser on this device.

Grid scale

13 pads ascend left to right, row by row, repeating the scale into higher octaves. Chord shapes stay relative to each pad’s root; they are not forced into the selected scale.

Your chord buttons

Use 1–6 whole numbers from 0 to 24, separated by commas. Include 0 for the root. For example, 0, 3, 7 makes a minor chord. Every button can have its own label and voicing.

Shape your sound

Attack fades in. Decay settles to the sustain level. Release fades out when you lift your finger. Without looping, notes end when the sample runs out. Changes apply to the next note.
